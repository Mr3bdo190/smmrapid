# SEO implementation

Implemented:
- Technical metadata: title, description, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card, robots.
- Arabic/English public SEO URLs with hreflang and x-default.
- Server-rendered metadata injection for platform pages in production so crawlers/social previews do not depend only on React hydration.
- Dynamic `/sitemap.xml` with public pages and platform-language URLs.
- Crawl controls in `public/robots.txt`; private dashboards and APIs are disallowed.
- JSON-LD for WebSite, Organization, Service, FAQPage and public service ItemList where appropriate.
- Public, indexable service catalog.
- Dedicated useful landing pages for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Telegram, Spotify, X/Twitter and Threads.
- Arabic and English keyword/topic clusters written naturally into relevant pages rather than hidden keyword stuffing.

Important: SEO cannot guarantee a #1 position. Google explicitly warns against keyword stuffing, doorway pages, scraped content and large volumes of low-value generated pages. The pages above are intended to be useful landing pages, not keyword-only pages.
