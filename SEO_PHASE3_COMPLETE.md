# RapidSMM SEO Phase 3

Implemented as a dedicated SEO layer on top of the working production build.

## Search appearance
- Brand-first homepage title: RapidSMM | SMM Panel & Social Media Marketing Services
- WebSite structured data with name RapidSMM and alternateName SMM Rapid / Rapid SMM
- Organization, WebPage and FAQ structured data on the homepage
- Server-side meta injection for public routes so crawlers receive correct title, description, canonical and JSON-LD in initial HTML

## Content strategy
- Dedicated /smm-panel landing page
- Dedicated /social-media-marketing-services landing page
- Platform pages for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Telegram, Spotify, X/Twitter and Threads in English and Arabic
- Internal links between commercial hubs and platform pages
- Unique titles/descriptions per page

## Technical SEO
- Canonical URLs
- hreflang alternates
- robots.txt with sitemap declaration and private-route exclusions
- XML sitemap covering all public commercial pages
- max-image-preview/max-snippet/max-video-preview robots directives
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata
- Semantic headings and visible content on SEO pages

## Important
Google controls the final search result site name and title. Structured data and consistent visible branding strongly indicate RapidSMM, but no implementation can force a particular Google result.
